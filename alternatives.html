Short answer

  • DetoxScreen: free, no ads, no account. Usage limits, schedules, limited opens, focus sessions, website blocking, location-based blocking and a strict mode that stops you from deleting the app while a block is active.
  • ScreenZen: free, funded by donations, built around adding a pause before an app opens. A good choice if gentle friction is enough for you.
  • Apple Screen Time: built into iOS and free, but a limit can be dismissed with "Ignore limit" in one tap.
  • Opal, one sec, Jomo, Freedom, AppBlock: polished apps with a free tier, but their most useful features usually require a subscription.

How to choose

If you just want a reminder, Apple Screen Time or a friction app like ScreenZen or one sec can be enough.

If you keep bypassing your limits, look for a real block with a strict mode. In DetoxScreen, strict mode means blocks can be tightened but not loosened, and the app cannot be deleted while a block is running.

If your distractions depend on where you are, location-based blocking helps: block games at the office or social media at school, automatically.

If you don't want another subscription, DetoxScreen and ScreenZen are the two free options with no ads.
